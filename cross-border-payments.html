Cross-Border Payments

Enabling Seamless Payments Across Borders

Expand your business globally with TPSL's Cross-Border Payment Solutions. Accept payments from international customers through a secure and reliable payment infrastructure, simplifying overseas collections and supporting business growth.

Key Features

International Payments, Simplified

  • International Payment Acceptance – Accept payments from overseas customers using international credit cards.
  • Simplified Settlements – Enable settlement of international collections into designated bank accounts through supported arrangements.
  • Secure Transactions – Facilitate cross-border payments through a secure payment infrastructure with appropriate risk and compliance controls.
  • Easy Integration – Integrate international payment acceptance into your existing online payment ecosystem.
  • Transaction Visibility – Access payment information and reports for better tracking and reconciliation.
