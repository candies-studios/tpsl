RBI MNRL and Customer Complaint Compliance Rules

Stay Informed. Stay Protected.

TPSL is committed to promoting customer awareness and compliance with applicable regulatory guidelines related to commercial communications and customer complaint registration.

Managing Commercial Communications

Customers can block all commercial communications or selectively manage their communication preferences through:

  • Their Telecom Service Provider's app or website
  • TRAI DND App
  • Calling or sending an SMS to 1909
Important Information

TPSL ePayments India Private Limited and TPSL India Private Limited will use the designated 1600XXX and 1400XXX numbering series for service and promotional calls, as applicable.

How to Register a Complaint

Report spam, fraud or cybercrime

Receiving Spam Despite DND Registration

Register a complaint through your Telecom Service Provider's app or website, the TRAI DND App, or by calling or sending an SMS to 1909.

Reporting Suspected Fraudulent Communications

Report suspected fraudulent communications received within the last 30 days through the Department of Telecommunications' Chakshu platform.
Chakshu Platform: https://sancharsaathi.gov.in/sfc/

Reporting Financial Fraud or Cybercrime

If you have experienced financial fraud or cybercrime, immediately contact the national cybercrime helpline at 1930 or register a complaint through the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal.
Cyber Crime Portal: https://cybercrime.gov.in/
