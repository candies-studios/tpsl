About TPSL

Your Trusted Payments Technology Partner

With over 26 years of experience, TPSL is a leading omnichannel payments technology company enabling seamless payment experiences for businesses across India.

A preferred technology partner to over 30 leading public and private sector banks, TPSL serves businesses across industries, including NBFCs, insurance, e-commerce, retail, hospitality, startups and more.

Our comprehensive suite of in-store and online payment solutions helps businesses accept payments efficiently, enhance customer experiences and grow with confidence.
